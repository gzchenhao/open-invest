# Case Study: How Manufacturing Company Transformed Compliance Management with AI

## Executive Summary

**Client**: Mid-sized manufacturing company with operations across 3 states
**Challenge**: Overwhelmed compliance management and missed opportunities
**Solution**: OpenInvest AI Policy Agent implementation
**Results**: 85% reduction in compliance time, $1.8M in discovered subsidies

## Company Background

**Industry**: Precision Manufacturing
**Employees**: 250
**Locations**: California, Texas, Arizona
**Annual Revenue**: $50M
**Compliance Team**: 2 full-time professionals

The company specializes in aerospace component manufacturing with a focus on advanced materials and precision engineering. As a government contractor, they must comply with numerous federal, state, and local regulations.

## The Challenge: Compliance Chaos

### Pain Points Identified

**Manual Research Burden**
- Team spent 40+ hours weekly tracking policy changes
- Had to monitor 15+ different government websites
- Information was scattered across spreadsheets and emails

**Missed Opportunities**
- Unaware of $1.2M in available manufacturing subsidies
- Missed 3 major application deadlines in previous year
- No systematic approach to incentive identification

**Compliance Risks**
- Falling behind on regulatory reporting requirements
- Difficulty tracking changes in environmental regulations
- No early warning system for policy changes

**Resource Constraints**
- Limited compliance team bandwidth
- High consulting costs for external policy expertise
- Competing priorities within the organization

### Financial Impact

**Direct Costs**
- $120,000 annual salary for compliance staff
- $80,000 in consulting fees
- $250,000 in missed subsidies

**Indirect Costs**
- Project delays due to compliance uncertainty
- Management time spent on compliance issues
- Reputational risk from regulatory violations

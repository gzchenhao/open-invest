Silicon Valley Quantum Computing Incentive Program
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

I. Program Overview
The Silicon Valley Quantum Computing Incentive Program aims to attract leading quantum computing companies to establish operations in Silicon Valley and foster innovation in quantum technologies.

II. Eligibility Requirements
1. Company must be registered in California
2. Primary business focus on quantum computing technology
3. Minimum investment of $5 million in quantum R&D
4. Employment of at least 25 full-time employees
5. Demonstration of proprietary quantum technology

III. Financial Incentives

A. Research & Development Tax Credit
- 25% tax credit on qualified quantum R&D expenditures
- Maximum annual credit: $2 million per company
- Credit carry-forward period: 5 years

B. Infrastructure Grant
- Up to $10 million for quantum computing facility construction
- Equipment financing at 0% interest for 5 years
- Free high-speed internet connectivity for 3 years

C. Workforce Development Grant
- $5,000 per employee for quantum skills training
- University partnership funding for quantum education programs
- Visa sponsorship support for international quantum experts

IV. Technology Requirements
1. Development of quantum algorithms or quantum hardware
2. Patent portfolio with minimum 3 quantum-related patents
3. Collaboration with accredited research institutions
4. Participation in quantum industry standards development

V. Compliance Requirements
1. Data localization for all quantum research data
2. Export control compliance for quantum technologies
3. Regular reporting to Quantum Technology Review Board
4. Security clearance for sensitive quantum research

VI. Application Process
1. Submit preliminary application by March 31, 2024
2. Provide detailed business plan and financial projections
3. Demonstrate quantum technology capabilities
4. Background check and security review
5. Final approval by Quantum Investment Committee

VII. Required Documentation
- Business registration documents
- Financial statements for past 3 years
- Quantum technology patent documentation
- Employee qualification records
- Security compliance plan
- University partnership agreements
